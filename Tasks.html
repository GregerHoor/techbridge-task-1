ABOUT THE INTERNSHIP

Your Learning Journey, Clearly Defined

The TechBridge internship is a structured 30-day practical learning experience designed to help participants develop their web development skills through eight progressive tasks. Each task introduces a new challenge, helping you move from fundamental website development to building a complete internship platform.
